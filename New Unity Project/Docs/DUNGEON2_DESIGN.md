# Hallow's Creek: the second dungeon (levels 16-18; designed 2026-10-15)

**Status: PROVISIONAL. Design only; nothing built, nothing run.** Written for Chris's brief of 2026-10-15 01:05 ("work on new
dungeons") while he was away. Every decision it needs from him is in section 9. It keeps the standing brief (DUNGEON_DESIGN.md
section 0: five players, a mix of the three classic dungeons in spirit only, no machines) and the rules the later dungeons share
(DUNGEON_DESIGN.md, "The three later dungeons: the rules they share"). Lessons only: no World of Warcraft names, layouts or assets.
British English. Every name below is labelled in section 7.

**Why this place.** The three dungeons already designed (the Sludge-Tunnels 18-20, the Deep Veins 23-25, the Transit Hub 27-29) all
stand in Argentis, which is not built. Hallow's Creek stands in the world that exists: it opens off Oakhaven's east road, where the
ruts run into the grey (the Road's End, the Hallow's Creek milestone and Bet's letter are already in the game). It needs no city
zone, so it is the next dungeon that can be built. It is **a built place, not a cave web** (the Adit's lesson, ADIT_REWORK.md 16-18):
a frozen town walked at street level, with its heights on stairs (a granary's lofts, a library's gallery, a bell tower), one chasm
crossed on hanging stone, and one short cellar.

## 1. The premise (in the books' terms)
Fifty years before the books, the Wasting began at Hallow's Creek: the Grand Mystic's working in the town square broke, and the
valley was paused mid-second (CANON, b1 ch.17-21). The town stands perfect and grey: white fences, a baker in his doorway with a tray,
a ball hanging in the air, and every townsperson stone to the skin with living eyes ("Time-Lost Echoes", aware, CANON). Where the
town hall stood is a glassy crater with a red door floating at its heart (CANON: the Old Archive's only anchor; the game never
reaches it). The Council calls it a blight and keeps the valley shut (CANON: "the official history... lies"). In the game's now, shortly
before Book 1 (shared rules), the High Council Investigation Bureau (CANON: the black sunburst; the game's Oakhaven ledger) holds the
town with a company of the Iron Pact (CANON army) and hired grey-pickers: it **cuts memory-quartz from the paused** (CANON-EXPANDED:
aether-geodes are fossilised memory, b1 ch.3; the paused are stone that remembers) and carts it west. Canon says the shell broken
kills the soul (b1 ch.17): what walks after the cutters' chisels is **the Hollow** (CANON creature; this origin GAME-ONLY). The
Bureau's Examiner has learned the town's one trick: a bell rung at the frozen minute wakes the square for sixty seconds (CANON: the
shop bell's Glitch, b1 ch.21; the great bell is GAME-ONLY), and every Waking Hour lets the grey breathe a field wider (GAME-ONLY: the
Rim's Reach-Stones have moved). The Preservationists (CANON: Vala Solari's guild, allies in the game) once kept the town's library (CANON,
b1 ch.18); their Warden who went to see why the grey moved is held there. The party goes in to free her, to take the Bureau's tally
as proof, and to silence the bell. The Spire, the Prism and the Archive's vault are Book 1's and are never touched.

## 2. The place: seven wings, in the leader's order
Open ground under a grey twilight (Khaven's dusk, no sun), the petrified wood as the walls, every way 12-14 m wide; floors in metres
above the yard; "+n" is a floor reached by stairs. Elites: 15 (section 7 lists every name's label; every hold reaches past the tank).

1. **The Ash Road** (required, ~8 min). The yard at the milestone, then 150 m of bleached cobbles north between stone trees whose
   shadows lie across the road in long bars (the hungry shadows, section 4). Pickets of grey-pickers and Pact soldiers; the
   **Fossilised Farm** off the west side (a stone house with a loft, a byre, the stone dog in the yard that is never fought):
   **Road-Captain Everard Hurst** holds it with his carters ("Down in the ash!": a 2 s knockdown within 5 m, every 14 s; drops the
   Road-Captain's Grey Cloak). Rare (one visit in four): **the Old Ram** of Bet's letter, grey and light as paper, wakes at the party's
   step and charges (disorient 3 s). Secret: a traveller's satchel under the rock overhang (a Pact soldier's letter about the shadows).
   The reveal: the road ends at a lip, and the chasm opens below with the bridge hanging in pieces over purple fog.
2. **The Bridge of Shattered Glass** (required, ~7 min). The old bridge was blown apart and the blast froze (CANON): its slabs hang in
   a jagged arch across 60 m of chasm. The party crosses on them: six slabs stepping down to -10 and up again, each joined by a short
   stone ramp (no slab edge steeper than 25 degrees), two of them broad enough to fight on (the Tables, 12x9 m). The far end is a built
   **bridge-head gatehouse** with a wall-walk at +5: **Sergeant Wymond Tredway** of the Pact holds its gate ("Hold the span!": roots
   the member he names 3 s, every 12 s; his shield-wall at half; drops the Span-Holder, a shield). Hidden, full runs only: a jump down
   from the third slab to the lower orbit, where **the Fallen Mason** (a Hollow, the man who built the bridge) waits on his own slab
   with a cache (a "Mortar" ring that roots), and a stair of small slabs climbs back to the fifth. Nobody falls under the world: the fog
   at -36 puts a fallen member back on the last slab (section 5, N3).
3. **Main Street** (the hub; required, ~9 min). 130 m of picket fences and white houses, every door and window stone, the paused on
   their doorsteps (standing figures with no agent: the crowd rule). The Bureau's **cutter gangs** work on three of them with chisels
   and sacks (decision 5); Pact patrols walk the length; **Assessor Piers Upcott** chalks the paused for cutting from the laundry yard
   ("Marked for the cut": the member he names is paused, stone for 3 s, every 15 s; drops the Assessor's Chalk, an off-hand that marks
   a target for the party). Rare (one visit in five): **the Lamplighter**, a Hollow who walks the street at the hour lighting dead lamps
   ("Lights out": fear 4 s within 6 m). Side rooms: **the bakery** (a Glitch room, section 4; the trapdoor to the Cellars), **the Reed
   house** (Tamsin's quest: her grandmother in the doorway with the other kettle) and **Bet's cart**, packed and stone, in a yard (a
   secret). The creek crosses the north end, grey and knee-deep (wading), with a footbridge east to the Granary road. The street opens
   on **the Square**: the crater's rim, a ring walk 16 m wide round a glass bowl 48 m across, the red door hanging at its heart, chairs
   and bricks drifting round it. **This is the reveal.** The well-house stands on the west rim, the stone willow on the east, the Bell
   Tower shut on the north, the Old Archive's steps on the north-east.
4. **The Granary on the race** (optional; standard and full runs; ~10 min). The town's tithe barn across the creek, 40 x 18 m, 10 m
   high, two lofts (+4 north, +8 south) by stairs, the race running under its north wall. The pickers' works: the sorting floor where
   memory-quartz is sized into Bureau crates, the wagon yard with the black-iron wagon and its two mules. The party fights up the stairs
   (the ramp lesson, ADIT_REWORK L3): **Picker-Master Fulke Yelland** on the north loft ("Bag him": a sack over the head of the member he
   names, disorient 3 s, every 14 s; "Sort them!" rallies the floor at half; drops the Picker's Gloves and the Picker-Master's Tally).
   **Gil Coldwell, the Loft Watch**, on the far loft with a net-bow (roots a named member 3 s; drops the Loft Watch's Net-Bow). The reveal:
   the tally ("Forty paused, cut. Yield twelve stone. To the coast station.") is the Salt-Menders' proof. The yard's south gate is the
   wagon track home (section 3); its bar lifts from the yard side only. Yelland down means no Pact wave on the Warden's walk (E2).
5. **The Cellars** (optional; full runs; ~7 min). Under the west houses, from the bakery's trapdoor: 150 m of brick-vaulted cellar
   (a cavern prop, style "cellar", 7 m wide, 3.5 high, floor -5 to -7, lamplit) where the cutters throw what they have broken. The Hollow
   come out of the walls' niches (the living-cave wing, in brick). **First-Cut**, the first they cut, in the Cut-Store (the collective
   shriek: fear 3 s within 6 m, every 20 s; his kin pour from the niches at half; drops the Cutter's Chisel, a dagger, and the Grey
   Quartz Ring). Hidden, in the one cellar with no lamp: **the Long Shadow**, a hungry shadow with a shape ("a minute taken": holds
   whoever stands in its shadow 4 s, telegraphed; drops a Minute in Amber, a trinket). The far stair comes up in the well-house on the
   Square's west rim and the trapdoor bars behind: the cellars are never walked back. First-Cut down means no Hollow wave on E2.
6. **The Old Archive** (required, ~9 min). A First Era library (CANON: columns twisted like pulled taffy, a crest of an open book with
   an eye, doors blown in, a maze of petrified shelves): steps up from the Square to a doorless front, the reading hall 50 x 24 m and
   12 m high at +2, its shelves standing 3 m high as inner walls (the maze lesson, L9: lamp rings at every turn), a gallery at +5 on
   three sides by the apse stairs, the Bureau's copying cells off the north wall. Packs of **Bureau clerks** who ward and mend each
   other and fight to the end (the zealots' lesson). **Registrar Dunstan Pardew** at the apse desk ("Hush": a 2 s stun within 5 m, every
   16 s; "Copy that" at half: two clerks released from the cells; drops the Registrar's Robe and the Ink-Stained Gloves). Optional: **the
   Reader at the Desk**, a paused Archivist in the stacks who wakes when the party passes ("Read to me": disorient 3 s on a named
   member; drops the Reader's Lens). The Warden's cell (a pen gate, "Lift the bar") holds **Warden Sigrid Elvet** of the Preservationists.
   Secret: a page of the High Archivist's log in the stacks (paraphrased; the vault, the riddle and the Prism are Book 1's). Freed, she
   walks (E2) to the Bell Tower's door and unbinds the Bureau's ward-lock.
7. **The Bell Tower** (the finale; required, ~8 min). 12 x 12 m, four landings at +4.5, +9, +13.5 and +18 by stairs round the walls,
   the belfry open on arches over the crater, the great bell hanging frozen mid-swing with a painted dial a minute short of noon (the
   clock's works dropped, 0.05; decision 4). **Pact-Captain Rowena Kersey** holds the second landing ("Form the line": a 3 s root on
   everyone within 4 m, every 15 s; drops the Pact-Captain's Breastplate). **Bureau Examiner Thurstan Vowell** waits in the belfry
   (`boss:true`): "The hour" pauses everyone within 8 m for 3 s (every 18 s, warned by the bell's hum); at half he rings the bell and the
   **Waking Hour** begins (E3): colour comes back, the square below moves, and four paused ringers climb the stair against the party
   holding the belfry's stair-head; when the loop snaps, Vowell stands stone for 5 s and the party has him. Drops: the Examiner's
   Sunburst (a necklace) and the Examiner's Seal (a trinket), every time. The reveal: from the arches, the whole town, the crater and
   the door, and the grey beyond it moving.

## 3. The run
- **Quick** (`/dungeon quick`): road, bridge, street, Archive, the Warden's walk, the tower: 35 camps, about 39 minutes (1.1 min a camp,
  the Adit's measured pace). **Standard**: adds the Granary and the Reader: 44 camps, about 48. **Full**: adds the Cellars, the lower
  orbit and the rares when they are there: 54 camps, about 59. The shared rule's 45-60 holds for standard and full.
- **The way home: the wagon.** The black-iron wagon in the Granary yard, mules in the traces, driven by hand: "Climb onto the wagon" and
  the party stands at the milestone (the Adit's carriage ride, generalised: N7). The wagon track runs round the chasm's east head and is
  barred at the yard end (lifted from the yard side only, like the Adit's sump bar), so it is never a way in. A group that leaves
  early from the Square walks back over the slabs; a bar on the gatehouse's town side keeps the Pact behind it.
- **One-way bits:** the jump to the lower orbit (full runs; the climb back is a stair); the Cellars' far trapdoor (bars behind); the
  Warden's walk (once begun, the Archive's door shuts behind her). Nothing required is walked twice.
- **Respawns:** none while inside (as asked for the Adit, Q1; decision 11). Bossdoors and bars shut again on every visit.
- **Spirit stones:** two, 20 m from any camp: under the gatehouse's wall-walk, and by the stone willow on the Square's east rim.

## 4. Mechanics new to this dungeon (three; no machines)
1. **The hungry shadows.** Long shadows lie across the road, the street and the cellars (CANON: "an absence of time"; you lose a
   minute, or an hour). A strip on the floor: standing in it slows you (haste -40%) and ticks a little damage; the sims path round it
   (a navmesh area with a high cost). They never cover a whole way. At the Waking Hour they shift.
2. **The Waking Hour and the Glitches.** A paused townsperson is a stone figure, no agent, no collider, a frozen first frame. A Glitch
   room (the bakery) or the great bell wakes them for 60-90 s: colour returns (a post-process swing), they move, some become a camp
   released by the event (`after`), and when the loop snaps anyone standing in a townsperson's ring (a 1.5 m circle, warned for 3 s) is
   paused 3 s. The bakery's Glitch is optional: its door, a stun on nobody, two waves of Hollow up the trapdoor, and two coppers left on
   the counter for a Discovery. The paused are never attacked by the party: the released camps are Hollow and ringers.
3. **The pause.** The Bureau's hold (Upcott, Vowell): a member turns to stone for 3 s (a stun with the stone look). Every hazard in a room
   warns longer than its longest pause, and none starts while someone is held.

## 6. The sims: how five run it
- **Roles:** you and four others. A Warrior or Paladin sim tanks (or you); Mira heals (a Druid sim when you play the healer); three
  damage dealers, one with crowd control (a Rogue's sap or a Mage's hex for the second Table and the clerks' packs).
- **The leader's plan** (DungeonPlan): wing by wing, the nearest open wing by navmesh walk with the height rule, so from the Square it
  takes the Granary (flat, nearest by the footbridge) before the Archive in standard and full, and the Archive first in quick; regroups at
  the gatehouse after the slabs, at the well-house after the Cellars, at the Archive's steps before the Warden's walk, at the tower's
  door after it. Camps on the Tables are cleared slab by slab; the leader waits at the gatehouse gate on its own side and says what it
  wants ("opens when Sergeant Tredway falls").
- **The walk:** every party sim guards Warden Elvet as they guard Mother Quillet; the leader holds the party at her pace; the waves come
  to her.
- **Holds:** a paused sim waits it out (SimCompanion.HoldUntil); the tank keeps Vowell out of the arches' line (no knockbacks in the
  belfry); the party holds the stair-head through the Waking Hour and turns on Vowell the moment he stands stone (a marked window, as
  the Rock-Eater's rampage is).
- **Shadows:** the sims path round them; a sim caught in one steps out on its own (the navmesh cost).
- **The ride:** at the wagon the leader says "Up you get" and the five stand at the milestone (as the carriage).
