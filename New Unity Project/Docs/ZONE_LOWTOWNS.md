## 1. The premise (in the books' terms)
Argentis is the capital: the Crown on the mountain, white stone and gold, and **the Lowtowns** at its base, "the sprawling, shadowed
districts ... filled with industry and poverty" (CANON, world_bible.md Geography). **The Rust Market** is Sector 4, at the Forge
District's walls, a market that "did not sleep" under the smog from the High City's foundries (CANON, b1 ch.3:3-9). The poor light
their rooms with shards, "spent magical batteries" (CANON, b1 ch.3:133-137); the game's reading, already set by the Sludge-Tunnels'
design, is that the shards are memory sifted out of the foundry's slag, and the Lowtowns burn their own dead for light a shard at a
time (GAME-ONLY; DUNGEON_DESIGN.md, the Sludge's story).

**The thread the player follows here is the game's own:** Wren and the soldier's boy, taken from Oakhaven by Seeker's warrant ("Road
to Argentis. No toll: by Seeker's warrant", peaks.json; "So Wren and the soldier's boy are beyond us. For now", main.ashrim.4).
In the Lowtowns you learn where such children go: "sparked" children are snatched "from the dirty alleys and the gutters" (CANON, b1
ch.13:143), held a night at the Gate, then taken up to the Crown, beyond reach until the Transit Hub (25-28). The zone ends with
the trail going up the mountain and down the drains at once: up for the children, down the Outfall for the shards (the Sludge-Tunnels).

## 2. The place (about 480 m square; x east, z north)
Arrival from the west, by **Oakhaven's south road** (Chris, 2026-10-15): a fork off Oakhaven's South road, east of the Rim's turning,
leaves Oakhaven's south edge as "South-east road to Argentis (dangerous, 16-19)" and comes in at the Gate Ward. The Shore's last turn-in
sends you home to Oakhaven to take it (the breadcrumb at 15), so the trail of Wren and the soldier's boy starts where they were taken.
The mountain and the Crown fill the north-east sky (a backdrop, never reached: white stone and gold above the smog). The Forge
District's wall closes the east side. The old river ("the Chemical River"; never "the Blue Anduin", a name hazard) runs north to south
through the middle, black, slow, crossed by two brick bridges.
- **The Gate Ward (west, the arrival):** Argentis's outer gate in the city wall, a toll-house, carters' yards, the **Holding House**
  where the warrant's children were kept a night (GAME-ONLY; the place the first chain finds them gone from).
- **The Rusty Spoon (CANON, b1 ch.1:9):** "a decrepit establishment in the Lowtowns known for watered-down ale and high-stakes
  games". The zone's inn (Phase 8.6's double door and a taproom from the start). The Raid Bell's tavern is a later book's; not here.
- **The Tenement Rows (centre and south):** narrow brick terraces four storeys high, washing lines across the lanes, stoops, yards,
  the "freezing puddles of questionable origin" (CANON, b1 ch.1:143) and puddled alleys; a chapel of no particular god turned soup-hall.
- **The Rust Market (Sector 4, east, under the Forge District's wall; CANON):** covered stalls in a tangle of lanes ("roasting
  skewered-rat", "cheap aether-spiced wine", b1 ch.3:7), the **Blind Seer's Antiquities** shopfront (CANON, b1 ch.3:13; here an
  honest-looking shop, its back room not entered), the **Iron Teeth's warehouse** (CANON; neutral ground up here, as the Sludge's
  design has it: they are fought below, not in the market).
- **The Shard-Burners' Yard (south-east):** the Lowtowns' dead-house, where the poor buy back a shard of their own (GAME-ONLY reading).
- **The Docks (the river's west bank):** "honest labor at the docks" (CANON, b1 ch.3:17); barges poled, not driven (no machines),
  cranes left out (machines), sacks and bales by hand.
- **The Drain Courts (north-west):** closed courts round old drain-mouths of red clay brick (the sewers' brick is CANON, b1 ch.9:47),
  where the Weave-Eaters come up at night.
- **The Outfall's mouth (south-east corner, where the Forge District's drain meets the river):** the Sludge-Tunnels' door (18-20,
  DUNGEON_DESIGN.md). Until that dungeon is built: a grating with a "dangerous, 18-20" sign and a quest that ends at it.
- **The Forge District gate (east wall):** shut; it opens with the Forge District zone (19-22).
- **The chimney stacks** stand over the wall (never "Blacksmoke Spire"); the smog lies over the whole zone by day (fog), orange
  fires and lamp-light by night.

## 5. Quests (QUEST_DESIGN.md rules; levels 16-19)

## 6. Trades (tier 6, 16-19; GAME-ONLY names)
- **Ore:** salvage iron from the ruined tenements and the river's bank (**slag-iron**: the foundry's cast-offs); a new crafting tier 6
  (Phase 8.6's tiers: copper, bog-iron, ridge-steel, ash-steel, veridian, then slag-iron 16-19).
- **Timber:** old beams from fallen tenements (**cinder-beam**). **Herbs:** gutter-growth (**soot-wort**, **lamp-lichen** near the
  drains, the rare **grey-bell** at the Shard-Burners' Yard). **Hides:** gutter-hound hides (Leatherworking). **Cloth:** the docks'
  sacking and the market's dyed stuffs (Weaving).
- **Stations:** a forge in the market (a smith's hearth; bellows are hand-worked, so not a machine), the Rusty Spoon's kitchen, a
  dyer's vat in the Rows.
