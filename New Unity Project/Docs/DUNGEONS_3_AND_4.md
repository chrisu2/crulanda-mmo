# Dungeons 3 and 4: the two after Hallow's Creek (stub; 2026-10-15)

**Status: PROVISIONAL.** One page so the four dungeons of Chris's brief (DUNGEON_DESIGN.md section 0: four in all; bands low 10-15,
mid 15-25, high 25-35) are on paper together. The first two are the Sealed Adit (10-12, being reworked: ADIT_REWORK.md) and Hallow's
Creek (16-18, DUNGEON2_DESIGN.md). The two below are already designed in full in DUNGEON_DESIGN.md (2026-10-14) and wait for Argentis
and the level cap; nothing here changes them. Labels: CANON, CANON-EXPANDED, GAME-ONLY, PROVISIONAL.

## Dungeon 3: the Deep Veins (mid band, levels 23-25; under the Warren District of Argentis)
- **Premise.** The penal legion's mine under the Lowtowns, "the dead roots of the entire mountain" (CANON, b1 ch.9), where the Council
  sends its unwanted: dissidents bound in stone-flesh and left in the walls as living faces (CANON, the Hall of Silent Faces), debtors
  given "seven years in the deep dark" (CANON), and now a gaol and holding pens being cut for ten thousand (the pens CANON, b1 ch.24;
  their cutting now GAME-ONLY). The party goes down the urchins' back way (the Blind Crow, blue chalk: CANON), past the faces and the
  Deep-Rats' toll (CANON: the Toll-Collector, who must live for Book 1), through the mine, frees the gaol (the Rising: cells, a ram-gang
  escort, a siege at a gate), breaks into the overseer's keep and finds the empty pens. Its lord, the Count-Master (GAME-ONLY), sells
  convicts to the foundry and holds a writ under Aethelgard's seal (CANON name, on a seal only).
- **Shape.** A rubble-stone undercroft, the basalt drains, a built gaol block and an overseer's keep: tunnels and built halls and a
  stone stronghold. Walked heights on the gaol's tiers and the keep's stairs; one gas pocket lit by a carried lantern (its one owner).
- **Canon it draws on.** b1 ch.9 (the whole way down, in its order), ch.3:133 (the legion's ore), ch.24:21 (the pens), ch.6 (the
  Warrens), world_bible (the goblin Weavers). Givers GAME-ONLY until Chris says Razzle, Rill and Chieftain Grohl may give quests.
- **Where it is written.** DUNGEON_DESIGN.md, "The Deep Veins" (sections 1-9). Built when the cap passes 22.

## Dungeon 4: the Transit Hub (high band, levels 27-29; the High City's south wall, over the Half-Light)
- **Premise.** "The junction for every soul-prism and aether-geode entering the High City" (CANON, b1 ch.1): a platform of iron and
  marble halfway to the clouds where the deep mines' freight is weighed, warded and written before it goes up to the Crown, and where
  children taken from Oakhaven went up in black transit-caskets (the caskets CANON; Wren and the soldier's boy the game's thread). The
  party climbs from the Half-Light's slum (CANON: Nila Glass-Stitcher lives there) up the porters' stair, across the Company's deck and
  through the Concord's halls (the Weigh-House, the Ink-House and its Census Vault) to the Crown's gate. Seekers, Inquisitors and
  Redaction (CANON) are placed here and nowhere else. Its last boss, Sovereign-Warden Cassian Mordaunt (GAME-ONLY), carries the Writ of
  Carriage that opens the Spire's cargo door to the raid (the Spire CANON; the door GAME-ONLY).
- **Shape.** A climb: the stair, an open sky deck (or a roofed hall with a painted drop, Chris's choice), the concourse and its three
  wings, the Crown stair. Rope rides worked by people (never "lift", 0.05). The story chain's end: the factor's name, the writ's order.
- **Canon it draws on.** b1 ch.1:161-227 (the Hub, the Half-Light, Nila, the caskets), ch.7:211-225 (the soldier's boy), ch.12:19-29
  (the Indentured, shown not played), ch.13 (Inquisitors), ch.15:183 (Seekers), world_bible (the Crown, the Silver Foundation).
- **Where it is written.** DUNGEON_DESIGN.md, "The Transit Hub" (sections 1-9, with seven questions for Chris). Built when the cap
  passes 29.

## The spare: the Sludge-Tunnels (mid band, levels 18-20; the Forge District's sewers and foundry)
Designed in full on 2026-10-14 (DUNGEON_DESIGN.md, "The Sludge-Tunnels"): the red-brick sewers (CANON, b1 ch.9), the Bone Cathedral
and the Cult of Ash (CANON, ch.10), the foundry and the Indentured's collars (CANON, ch.12), the Iron Teeth's counting-house (the
syndicate CANON; the house GAME-ONLY). With Hallow's Creek at 16-18 the mid band holds three dungeons. **Chris decides** (DUNGEON2_DESIGN.md
decision 1): five dungeons, or four with the Sludge-Tunnels folded (its ledger link, "forty, spent" and the factor's mark, moves into
the Deep Veins' approach; the collars, the Spent and the Cult of Ash then go unused in any dungeon).
