## Canon differences from the original brief
Searched all of `D:\code\crulanda`: **Forgeborn is canon** (Tynara; "one of the few who could manipulate metal with
minor telekinesis"; Forgeborn tinkers in the Rust District / Sector 9). **Driftkin, Windcallers, Shatterlings and
stormglass do not appear anywhere** in the lore folder, so the Windcaller class and Driftkin race from the brief are
unsupported by the available sources (they may come from material not in this folder).
Canon factions there include the Alliance (Salt-Menders), High Concord, Sandthrone, Glass Fleet, Veridian Keepers,
Silent Pilgrims, Silver Remnant and the Weavers of the Warrens (goblins). The class matrix and talent trees still use the
brief's candidate classes; reconcile them with the author before locking the roster.
Style pillars from the world bible that apply to the game: gritty 80s/90s high fantasy, no anachronistic tech words,
mythic folk texture, cosmic "Pale Things", and the Wasting as unmaking rather than burning.
