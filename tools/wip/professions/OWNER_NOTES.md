# Owner's requests and decisions for professions, gathering and the village economy (2026-10-01)

Verbatim, in order:
1. "we need to gather ore, lumber,herbs etc and sell or refine. also have a profession for the character. blacksmith, alchemist, cook, etc."
2. Decisions by question prompt: **two crafting professions, gathering free for everyone, Cooking free**; first crafts **Blacksmith,
   Alchemist, Cook**, each at a village workplace.
3. "have the leatherworker make bags for professions by quest (gather leathers) or just buy them outright from him. so he can buy
   food/lumber for his family. each npc has their own house and each profession has their own workshop."
   Read back to the owner and not corrected: bags for gathered materials (ore sack, herb pouch...) by quest or purchase; every
   tradesperson has a purse and visibly spends it (bread, firewood) for the household; one named house per household (families
   share); a workshop per trade (leather shop apart from the tannery yard, herbalist's drying hut = alchemy bench, hunter's
   lodge, an innkeeper and a kitchen at the inn = cooking hearth).
4. "this also means all animals are huntable for their leather"
   Read back and confirmed: **wild animals become huntable** (deer, rabbits and the like: targetable, killable, they still bolt,
   they come back over time) and **every beast can be skinned** for its own hide or pelt (deer, rabbit, wolf, boar, stag...),
   which feed the leatherworker's bag quest and sell to him and the skinner.
5. "farm animals stay . not huntable": **chickens, sheep and the village cats are never huntable.**

Also asked the same day (done): "have unemployed npc show up at the inn and drink till gone or passed out" (the drinkers,
`VillageDrinkTests`), and whether eggs can be bought at the merchant (yes, after the hen-wife's afternoon delivery).
