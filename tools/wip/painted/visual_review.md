# Visual review of the published painted pass (2026-10-01): ranked worklist

Merged the four reviews into 14 items, cheapest high-impact first. I spot-checked 12 captures (oakhaven-01, -04, -11, -25, -31, -80-weather-storm, -95; ashrim-03; verdant-06, -88; peaks-07; khaven-20) and every top item is plainly visible in them. No code was re-read: causes, method names and line numbers are the reviewers' own, taken from a working tree that includes the uncommitted masonry patch. Nothing was modified or run.

The wilds reviewer's JSON arrived cut off partway through its caves item. Its items 6-10 exist only as one-line titles from its summary: Mossveil Falls, seven landmark shots that show nothing of the place, exit waystones, flat discs for mud/puddles/fire beds, and the ruler-straight moss line on giant trunks plus the Fallen Ghost-Oak. The caves fix below is therefore partial, and the "seven empty landmark shots" could not be merged because the files were never named.

Items 1-5 are number or data changes, or small code edits in one place, and together change the look of nearly every Oakhaven and Verdant frame. Items 6-8 are small prop and ground fixes. Items 9-14 are medium or large shape work.

Left off the list as lower gain per effort:
- Mossveil Falls flat sheet and boulder grid (medium; the night glow is covered in item 5)
- streaky clouds (medium)
- Peaks far range as a flat fog-coloured cutout (medium)
- bakehouse oven, mill wheel, identical cottage silhouettes (medium each)
- salt-flat squiggles, neon Peaks ferns, Khaven's all-rose fog (small, low impact)
- moss line on giant trunks, flat mud/puddle discs (no detail received)

Dropped as in progress: stone texture on towers, walls, keeps, ruins, forge, oven plinth and waystone.

## 1. [high impact, small] Oakhaven daylight is milky: pale neutral fog and a white horizon wash out the village
Captures: oakhaven-01-entrance.png, oakhaven-03-the-golden-cask.png, oakhaven-17-brook-pond.png, oakhaven-30-the-watchtower.png, oakhaven-25-the-cider-barn.png
Problem: From the entrance, the village, the Great Oak and the tree lines sit behind a grey-white veil, and the lower sky is nearly white. The picture splits into a vivid near half and a washed far half. Verdant uses similar distances but a saturated teal fog, so there distance reads as coloured air.
Cause: EncounterContent/Zones/oakhaven.json, lighting block: fogColor #AFC0C6 is near-neutral, fogStart 70 and fogEnd 240 put the village at 20-45% fog, and skyExposure 1.2 with skyTint #788FB8 whitens the horizon. WorldClock.Apply also feeds the fog colour into the sky's _GroundColor.
Fix: Data only. Set fogColor to about #8FB4DC, fogStart to about 110, fogEnd to about 340, skyExposure to about 1.0 and skyTint to about #5C88CC. Re-check dawn and dusk, which blend from these values.

## 2. [high impact, small] Broadleaf crowns read as brown potatoes on sticks (Oakhaven, Verdant, Khaven)
Captures: verdant-06-mossveil-falls.png, oakhaven-01-entrance.png, oakhaven-21-the-mastwood.png, oakhaven-31-exit.png, verdant-02-the-ridge-of-long-shadows.png, verdant-03-the-verdant-ocean.png, oakhaven-08-the-old-orchard.png
Problem: Every ordinary broadleaf is a smooth brown or olive lump on a thin trunk with a few leaf cards on top. In verdant-06 the foreground tree is a brown rock with six yellow leaves. It is the most numerous object in the forests and spoils the giant trees and the Great Oak, which read well. Two reviewers reported it independently.
Cause: ZoneBuilder.Broadleaf calling LeafCrown (ZoneBuilder.cs, about lines 1441 and 1492). The core is one canopy blob scaled (4, 2.3, 4), as wide as the whole card crown, tinted Color.Lerp(Leaf[family], Color.black, .45f). For autumn families that is mid brown. The 10-14 card clusters sit on top and do not cover its flanks.
Fix: Minimum: shrink the core to about (2.6, 1.5, 2.6), tint it a deep leaf green or the family's card colour darkened at most 15% (never brown), and enlarge rim and side clusters by 30-40% (spot.size * 1.15). Better: replace the core with 4-6 overlapping leaf-coloured lumps, as the Great Oak does, and keep the cards as a ragged fringe. Apply the same to the orchard call and the backdrop silhouettes.

## 3. [high impact, small] Meadow reads as bright tufts on a dull grey-green carpet, with a cut line and hard tall-grass discs
Captures: oakhaven-01-entrance.png, oakhaven-04-communal-well.png, oakhaven-26-withy-pool.png, oakhaven-99-pond.png, oakhaven-18-the-tall-grass.png, oakhaven-08-the-old-orchard.png, peaks-07-umbra-scarp.png
Problem: The pasture is separate vivid yellow-green tufts over a flat, desaturated floor with large bare gaps. About 70 m out the tufts stop along a line. Tall-grass patches are perfect circles with a sharp rim and flat top. In the Peaks the same tufts are lime specks on olive.
Cause: ZoneBuilder.PaintGround meadow colours (grassA .22/.40/.16, grassB .35/.47/.19, multiplied by .88 and up) are far duller than the tuft materials tinted toward (.34, .6, .2) in Awake. Meadow density is 2.3 per square metre against Verdant's 2.8. GrassField.DrawDistance is 70 with whole 24 m cells switched on and off, no fade. The tall loop in GrassField.Build scatters uniformly over a disc at one height and tint.
Fix: - Raise the meadow paint to the tufts' lit colour (grassA about .26/.50/.18, grassB about .38/.60/.22; keep the gold patches).
- Raise meadow density to about 3.2.
- Fade tufts over the last 20 m, by distance scale in Grass.shader or dithered clip.
- In the tall loop, wobble the rim with noise, thin and shorten toward the edge, scatter a few outlier clumps and mix a second tint.
- Give the mountain alp colours the same match.

## 4. [high impact, small] Rain and storm do not darken the day
Captures: oakhaven-80-weather-storm.png, oakhaven-80-weather-rain.png, oakhaven-81-weather-storm-pond.png, verdant-80-weather-storm.png, oakhaven-80-weather-overcast.png
Problem: In Oakhaven and Verdant, overcast, rain and storm are nearly the same picture: light grey sky, a bright pale fog wall and grass as luminous green as in sunshine. Only the streaks change. The storm has no weight.
Cause: WorldClock.Apply: lift = 1 + (.14 * dim - .2 * rain) * lit lowers ambient by only about 8% in a storm. Weathered() darkens fog by only 14%. ZonePost cuts exposure by only .06 * rain. WorldWeather.LateUpdate darkens the cloud underside by only 28%.
Fix: Numbers only.
- WorldClock: rain term about -.45; fog x(1 - .4 * rain * lit) with a blue-slate cast; sky exposure x(1 - .25 * rain).
- ZonePost: exposure x(1 - .16 * rain * lit); saturation a further -.15 * rain.
- WorldWeather: cloud under colour x(1 - .5 * rain); storm fog .56 to about .45.
- If grass still glows, check that Grass.shader takes the ambient.

## 5. [high impact, medium] Night: windows and lamps bleach to cold white, the base is murky grey-navy, and unlit effects glow
Captures: oakhaven-95-village-night.png, verdant-95-village-night.png, oakhaven-94-coop-night.png, oakhaven-99-pond-night.png, verdant-99-pond-night.png, peaks-99-pond-night.png
Problem: Lit windows are flat white rectangles with no firelight colour and no glow on the wall or ground (oakhaven-95). Away from lamps the frame is near black, desaturated, with heavy dark corners. Mossveil Falls and chimney smoke stay at day brightness and bloom, so the waterfall is the brightest thing on screen at night.
Cause: ZonePost.OnRenderImage applies the night grade to the whole frame after tone mapping: saturation .62, tint (.86, .92, 1.12), exposure 1.2, bloom 1.1, threshold .55, vignette + dark * .4. The glass emission (1, .62, .25) x 1.3 clips white, then is desaturated and tinted blue in Post.shader fragComposite. WorldClock.Init's night Look has moon .26 and low grey ambient. Only the inn has point lights. FallMaterial (ZoneBuilder.Verdant.cs) and ZoneBuilder.Smoke use the unlit particle shader with fixed colours.
Fix: Do the first three bullets first; they are numbers only.
- ZonePost: night saturation about .9, exposure about 1.05, bloom about .8, threshold about .7, vignette capped at g.vignette + .15.
- WorldClock night: moon about .4 and bluer (.45, .6, 1); ambient sky about (.24, .32, .55), equator (.18, .24, .42), ground (.1, .12, .2).
- Post.shader: key the desaturation and cool tint off luminance so bright pixels stay warm.
- Glass emission at night about (1, .5, .14) x 2, driven by the lamp factor.
- One warm shadowless light (range about 6) per house outside its lit windows; lamp range about 11; inn dayIntensity about .5.
- Scale waterfall, mist and smoke tints by WorldClock.Darkness, as WastingStatic.Tint does.

## 6. [high impact, small] Gable ends of every roof wear slate or thatch instead of wall
Captures: oakhaven-04-communal-well.png, oakhaven-11-vells-smithy.png, oakhaven-97-trade-baker.png, khaven-04-the-cracked-hearth.png, khaven-97-trade-merchant.png
Problem: The triangle under the ridge at each end of every house, inn, barn and the well roof is covered in pale slates or thatch rows, as if the roof were a solid block of tiles. There is no bargeboard, so the roof ends in a raw cut. It is the clearest generated tell on every cottage.
Cause: ZoneMeshes.GableRoof (ZoneMeshes.cs lines 11-37) closes the roof with two gable triangles in the same mesh and material as the slopes. House, Inn and Barn add nothing behind them, and Eaves() (ZoneBuilder.cs about line 1080) adds only fascia, rafter ends and a ridge cap.
Fix: Give GableRoof a second submesh for the gable triangles, or drop them and add an inset triangle at the wall face, in the wall material (plaster or boards) with metre UVs. Add a king post and collar, and two bargeboards per gable in Eaves().

## 7. [medium impact, small] Hedges are flat dark boxes and haystacks are smooth beige eggs
Captures: oakhaven-04-communal-well.png, oakhaven-11-vells-smithy.png, oakhaven-03-the-golden-cask.png, oakhaven-07-harrow-farm.png, oakhaven-10-harrow-hen-coop.png, oakhaven-13-thornes-bakehouse.png, khaven-15-the-drowned-fields.png
Problem: Sharp-edged, uniformly dark green boxes stand by the well and the inn and look like untextured collision blocks. Every farm has one or more perfectly smooth pale ellipsoids for hayricks (nine in Oakhaven); the cart load is the same shape.
Cause: ZoneBuilder.BuildProps: case "hedge" (about line 951) is one primitive cube 6 x 1.4 x 1.2 m in a dark foliage tint. Case "haystack" (about line 952) is one primitive sphere scaled 2.6 x 2.2 x 2.6 in art.hay, with smeared UVs. Cart() (about line 2022) reuses the load.
Fix: Hedge: a run of overlapping Lump(Boulder()) canopy blobs in two or three greens lifted toward the grass, with a few leaf sprays; keep the collider. Haystack: a Turned() profile (flared skirt, shoulder, pointed cap) with thatch tiling in metres, a centre pole, loose tufts at the foot, and height and yaw varied by position. Use a squashed version for the cart load.

## 8. [high impact, medium] Ashrim: ground reads as paving, ash falls as hard black and white dots, and everything is one grey value
Captures: ashrim-03-the-unwoven-flats.png, ashrim-01-entrance.png, ashrim-13-the-walled-mouth.png, ashrim-18-exit.png, ashrim-87-secret-hunters-cache.png, ashrim-80-weather-ash-squall.png, ashrim-10-the-last-orchard.png
Problem: The whole zone wears one even net of same-sized polygons with uniform outlines, which reads as flagstones. The Unwoven Flats, meant to be unnaturally smooth, have the same tiling. Crisp black and white discs pepper every frame like sensor dust. Ground, fog and sky share one mid-grey, so distance is blank, and the ash squall looks like plain overcast. The grey, dead mood itself is right.
Cause: - Crust: ZoneBuilder.PaintGround ashen branch (about lines 457-471, 506-523) uses one Voronoi site per 5 m cell and draws every edge, fading only 25%. AshDetail (about line 632) repeats it at 0.6 m.
- Flakes: NatureFx.cs FallingLeaves.Init ash branch uses .2 and .92 greys at alpha .95, as round mote billboards.
- Values: ashrim.json fogColor #A3A1A8 matches the lit ground, with fogStart 22. WeatherLook.Of AshSquall (dim .52, fog .72) is close to Overcast.
Fix: - Crust: mask cracks with low-frequency noise to about 40% cover, make 55-65% of edges faint or absent, mix 3.5 m and 9 m plates, and fade cracks on slopes, roads and near trunks.
- Unwoven Flats: give the shape a crack-free paint.
- AshDetail: soften to grain.
- Flakes: greys about .45 and .8, alpha .5-.6, size .04-.14, an irregular soft sprite, emission scaled by WorldWeather.Now.ash.
- Fog: about #8C8796, fogStart about 35, ambientSky about #6E6A86.
- Squall: fog about .5, dun tint about .8 * ash, haze alpha about .4.

## 9. [high impact, medium] World edge at exits: dressing stops on a line, bare smeared slopes, single-leaf trees, box waystone
Captures: oakhaven-31-exit.png, verdant-14-exit.png, khaven-22-exit.png, peaks-16-exit.png, oakhaven-21-the-mastwood.png, oakhaven-06-oak-creek-mill.png
Problem: At every exit the grass, ferns and flowers stop on a ruler line. Beyond it is blurred, stretched ground paint rising into bare hills dotted with thin sticks that each carry one giant leaf card, 10-30 m from the camera. The exit waystone is a plain pale cuboid with a glowing cube on top. This is the frame the player sees when leaving a zone.
Cause: ZoneBuilder.BuildBackdrop: the skirt reuses mirrored ground paint on rings up to 14 m apart, and broadleaf silhouettes are three crossed cards of a single leaf cluster, with the first rows at 8 and 14 m. GrassField and PlantField scatter only inside the zone square. BuildForestEdge plants one thin line at Half - 5. The waystone builder is a box plus a cube; the masonry patch changes its texture only.
Fix: - Scatter GrassField and PlantField out to Half + 25 on the backdrop height, with density tapering.
- For rows 8 and 14, use the real Broadleaf and Pine builders without colliders (after the crown fix) and halve the step.
- Add skirt rings at 2.5 and 6 m.
- Give the waystone a tapered, leaning Cutout or Turned shape with a carved cap and a recessed glow.

## 10. [high impact, medium] Buildings on slopes: sunk barn doors, tall stone wedges, a ruin overhanging the hill
Captures: oakhaven-25-the-cider-barn.png, oakhaven-22-mosss-lodge.png, oakhaven-07-harrow-farm.png, oakhaven-93-coop-shut.png, oakhaven-06-oak-creek-mill.png, oakhaven-28-hollin-farm.png
Problem: Buildings stand level at their centre height while the ground slopes under them. The Cider Barn's door is cut off diagonally by the grass with a stone footing at one corner only (oakhaven-25). Harrow Farm's barn sits on a wedge over a metre tall. The mill's uphill wall is buried. The Hollin Farm slab overhangs the slope.
Cause: ZoneBuilder.Root() (about line 842) places props at Ground(p.at); nothing levels terrain for ordinary buildings (only ZoneShape pads in PrepareShapes). FootDrop (about line 855) only extends the plinth down. Barn() (about lines 1314-1329) puts its door at a fixed y with no LocalGround lookup and has no uphill plinth.
Fix: In the height function, blend to the prop's centre height inside Footprint() plus 1.5 m with a 3 m falloff, for house, inn, barn, mill and ruined_house (the mechanism the shape pads already use). Interim: give Barn() the House door treatment and an all-round plinth course.

## 11. [high impact, medium] Hero buildings are placeholders: the inn is a blank box, the smithy is posts under a flat slab
Captures: oakhaven-95-village-night.png, oakhaven-03-the-golden-cask.png, oakhaven-01-entrance.png, khaven-04-the-cracked-hearth.png, oakhaven-11-vells-smithy.png, oakhaven-97-trade-blacksmith.png
Problem: The Golden Cask and Cracked Hearth are 12 x 8 m boxes of flat plaster with one rail and rows of small identical windows; from the back the inn is a wall with eight squares. Vell's smithy, beside the Great Oak, is a thin flat board with slates more than twice house size on four posts, a chimney poking through, and a flat glowing rectangle for coals.
Cause: ZoneBuilder.Inn (about lines 1183-1283): five flat BoxParts, four corner posts, one storey rail, 0.9 x 0.8 m windows, no jetty, porch or studs. ZoneBuilder.Forge (about lines 2722-2758): the roof is a primitive cube 5.6 x .14 x 4.6 whose 0..1 UVs stretch one slate tile over it; hood, bellows and coals are single cubes.
Fix: - Inn: studs every 2.6 m and a sill rail per window row; jetty the upper front 0.3 m on brackets; a gabled porch on two posts; paired or larger upper windows; a dormer or two; a sign twice the size. Timber goes outside the walls so interior and colliders stay.
- Smithy: a lean-to roof mesh with metre UVs (slate at tile 2.5) on beams and rafters with a fascia; a tapered hood and chimney cap; a heap of coal lumps over the glow; a plank half wall.

## 12. [high impact, large] Ruined houses and ruins are rows of square-topped blocks
Captures: khaven-20-the-listeners-hut.png, khaven-02-blood-stone-well.png, khaven-03-the-gallows-tree.png, oakhaven-28-hollin-farm.png, peaks-04-ruined-waystation.png, peaks-11-the-sealed-adit.png, ashrim-06-cinderfold.png, ashrim-17-the-reach-stones.png
Problem: This is about shape, not the stone texture being patched. Fallen cottages are slabs of random height side by side, reading as battlements or toy bricks (khaven-20), in clean smeared plaster with no frame, openings, rubble or scorch; the fallen roof is a flat slab. The Sealed Adit and Walled Mouth are a few boxes. The Reach Stones are identical cuboids. Ashrim's ruin walls go near black.
Cause: ZoneBuilder.RuinedHouse (about lines 2169-2204) builds each wall stub as a 1.2 m primitive cube with random height in tinted plaster, so tops are level and the texture stretches. Ruin (about line 2133) is a row of 1.05 m boxes. peaks.json and ashrim.json use kind "ruin" for the Sealed Adit and the Walled Mouth. The Reach Stones are plain box props.
Fix: First step (medium): in RuinedHouse, build each wall run as one Cutout() polygon with a jagged sloping top from the same R01 draws and metre UVs; add a door gap, charred corner posts and a broken rail, soot on the top 0.3 m, a taller gable or chimney stub, and rubble at the foot (BoulderAt, Scree). Then: the same profile for Ruin plus toppled blocks; a small adit prop (timber frame, boards, dark recess, spoil heap); tapered leaning stones for the Reach Stones; lift the ash plaster tint a step.

## 13. [high impact, large] Peaks: steep ground is smooth grey putty, and crags are crumpled shards glued on top
Captures: peaks-07-umbra-scarp.png, peaks-06-the-high-ledge.png, peaks-01-entrance.png, peaks-12-the-cold-tarn.png, peaks-15-avalanche-scar.png, peaks-16-exit.png, khaven-08-carrion-cliffs.png
Problem: Every hump and gorge wall in the Peaks is a soft blurred grey dune with green airbrushed on top: no strata, cracks or edges. It fills half of most Peaks frames (peaks-07). The dark crag lumps on it are a different colour, flat-shaded into dozens of small triangles, and line up as a row of teeth. Loose boulders elsewhere are smooth and nearly white, making three rock styles.
Cause: ZoneBuilder.PaintGround mountain branch (about lines 490-505): rock and scree are two flat colours blended by low-frequency Perlin in a top-down paint that stretches on steep faces; BuildGround halves detail grain there. The PaintedRock shader is on props and the backdrop only. CragRock uses ZoneMeshes.Blob(seed, 1.25f, true, 9, 14, 6, 4, faceted: true); Cliff/Step stacks three every 2.2 m with random lean and yaw. Boulders use PaintedRock at _Scale 5 with _Top (1.18, 1.14, 1.02).
Fix: - Cheap first step (small): in PaintGround, modulate rockC by bedding bands keyed on HeightAt plus noise (0.6-1.3 m beds, dark foot line, lit lip), restore full grain on rock, and match the Cliff tint to the ground rock.
- Full fix: a slope-masked, world-projected painted-rock layer on the ground material (or a steep-triangle submesh with RockTint) fading out as normal.y goes from .75 to .9.
- Crags: fewer, larger planes with softer normals; yaw within 12 degrees and lean within 4 so lumps merge into ledged faces.
- Boulders: _Scale about 2.2, _Top about 1.08, tints about 15% darker.

## 14. [medium impact, medium] Caves are raw faceted triangles, and two Root-Mother's Deep shots are broken
Captures: verdant-88-hollow-drop.png, verdant-85-hollow-camp.png, verdant-89-hollow-stores.png, oakhaven-86-hollow-hall.png, oakhaven-88-hollow-drop.png
Problem: Cave walls are large flat triangles with knife-edge shards and almost no visible texture. The Root-Mother's Deep is one murky olive from floor to roof. verdant-88 shows the passage from outside: a tube mouth, a straight rectangular floor strip, surface ferns in frame and a pale hole in the shell. That is either a real gap at the sunk temple hollow or the camera standing outside. verdant-85 is a dark tunnel with no subject.
Cause: ZoneBuilder.Cavern (about line 3228): a deliberately faceted shell from 14 arch segments per ring with bumps up to about 38%, flat tints on art.stone or art.bark. RootDeepInterior (about line 3573) lights only with dim green Glow (range 7, intensity .55). EncounterCapture.cs lines 481-495 place the camp and drop views by heuristics tuned to Crowsfoot Hollow, which land at the Root Deep's exposed entrance stair.
Fix: The reviewer's fix was cut off in transit, so this is partial.
- First check in the editor whether verdant-88 is a real hole where the passage crosses the temple root-stair hollow (height -2.5). If so, close the shell or knoll there; otherwise give the Root Deep its own capture offsets.
- Shell: double the ring resolution, smooth the normals, use the painted rock material with a darker floor-to-wall gradient.
- Root Deep: add a few warm or contrasting lights so the space has shape.
