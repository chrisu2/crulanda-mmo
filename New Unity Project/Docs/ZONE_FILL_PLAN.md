# Oakhaven fill plan (note 172, Chris 13:12; after the publish)
Goal: the starting zone feels lived-in and wooded, like a classic forest starter zone. A point of interest every 100-150 m. No machines.
1. Forest: dense mixed-forest edges along the zone border, plus 6-8 groves (oak, birch, pine) with undergrowth. Instanced (GPU instancing, shared materials).
2. Roads: winding dirt roads joining the village, the farms, the lake and the mine mouth. Fences, signposts at every fork, wagons and crates along the verges.
3. Farmsteads: 3 outlying farms with fields (reuse CropField), a barn, a well and scarecrows.
4. Camps: a boar wallow and an outlaw camp (tents, a fire, crates) as level bands.
5. Water: a lake with reeds, and a stream crossing the road under a timber bridge.
6. A mine mouth: a timbered adit in a hillside, with carts pushed by hand and spoil heaps. No machinery.
7. Scatter: rocks, logs, stumps, flowers, mushrooms and bushes, instanced and density-capped per cell.
8. Wildlife: more deer, rabbits, birds and boars wandering between the POIs.
Per chunk: commit, frames of the area reviewed, FPS checked in the capture (keep the frame rate where it is now), push, backup.
