## 3. About This Game

The Great Oak is in full leaf, and the Golden Cask is warm. Two hundred and forty souls live in Oakhaven, and lately they keep their voices down. High Concord collectors hold the village. On the east road, where the grass turns grey, they guard a black-iron wagon they would rather you left shut.

Crulanda: The Quiet Trail is a single-player RPG in the classic MMO style. Open the wagon. Follow its ruts west. Find out where the children went.

In the east of Crulanda, the Wasting is eating the map. It does not burn. It unmakes, leaving grey static where farms and forests stood. Your trail runs to walled Khaven, where it is always dusk; over the Sandthrone toll gate in the Shattered Peaks; and down to the Ashland Rim, where the Ash-Walkers fight what comes out of the grey with salt.

- **Four zones, levels 1 to 10.** Camps respawn, elites hit hard, and wolves wait in the tall grass.
- **A village that keeps its own hours.** Smiths, bakers, millers and hen-wives work, gossip, run home when a fight starts, and go to bed at nightfall.
- **Day, night and weather.** Lamps come on after dark. Rain and storms cross the farmland, mist rises in Khaven, flurries blow through the Peaks, and ash squalls sweep in off the Wasting.
- **Quests the old way.** A ! over a head, a tracker, and a quest book that keeps every page you find, from a Bureau ledger to a lullaby.
- **Six factions,** among them the Salt-Menders and the Sandthrone, with standing from Hostile to Sworn.
- **Warrior or Druid.** Hold the line with sword and shield, or shift between the Druid's four forms: Barkhide, Thornclaw, Rootmend and Thornsong. Both have talent trees.
- **Water and woods worth stopping for.** Turquoise shallows over sand, creeks to wade, a pond to swim, and painted trees that let the sky through.

The Quiet Trail belongs to The Land of Crulanda, a saga in novels and games. The novels begin with The First Spoke. Whatever you learn in Oakhaven, the books remember.
